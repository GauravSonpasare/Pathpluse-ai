How PathPulse Works

A seamless four-step pipeline transforming fragmented pedestrian concerns into verified civic action and safer walking routes.

01

Capture

Snap a quick photo, drop a GPS pin, or dictate notes on broken sidewalks, missing covers, or unlit walkways while walking.

02

Analyze

Edge AI scans visual evidence, classifies hazard categories, and estimates risk impact based on pedestrian vulnerability and environment.

03

Prioritize

Our multi-factor scoring model ranks issues by urgency from 0–100, highlighting lethal choke points before accidents happen.

04

Resolve

Municipal teams and civic agencies get structured repair tickets, and commuters receive real-time notifications once verified safe.

Transparent, Actionable Data For Safer Cities

Vague complaints like "the street is broken" sit in municipal backlogs for months. PathPulse AI turns every report into a verified engineering ticket complete with severity scores, image classification, and precise coordinates.

  • Eliminates Subjective Bias: Algorithmic scoring ranks true hazards over squeaky-wheel complaints.
  • Instant Community Alerts: Pedestrians navigating nearby routes receive proactive hazard warnings.
  • Public Verification: Issues cannot be quietly marked closed until confirmed resolved on the ground.

Community Safety Impact

How It Helps

Empowering neighborhoods to identify, prioritize, and eliminate pedestrian hazards together.

Safer Daily Commutes

Pedestrians receive proactive hazard awareness for poorly lit corridors, missing grates, and tripping risks before stepping into danger, keeping evening walkers and school children safe.

Evidence-Based Repair

City workers and contractors don't have to guess where repairs are needed most. The 0–100 risk score establishes a clear, data-driven queue so life-threatening issues get fixed first.

Accessibility for All

Broken ramps, cracked curbs, and sidewalk obstructions disproportionately affect seniors, parents with strollers, and wheelchair users. PathPulse flags barrier-free alternative paths.

Civic Accountability

Public audit histories track timestamps from initial photo submission to repair dispatch, fostering transparency and trust between citizens and local governance.

Chronic Hotspot Detection

By aggregating recurring micro-reports over time, urban planners can identify structurally degraded corridors requiring long-term capital rebuilding instead of quick patches.

Neighborhood Empowerment

Transform passive frustration with broken sidewalks into active, collaborative civic stewardship. A five-second photo can prevent someone else's emergency room visit.
